## Description

Gym Tross is the mobile companion to your gym's management console.

For staff
- Scan a member's QR code to check them in, instantly
- Register members and take their photo
- Renew, freeze or cancel memberships; record payments and refunds
- See today's collection and who is behind on dues
- Message a member or walk-in, and review what was sent

For members
- See your membership status and expiry date
- Show your QR code at the door

Your gym's data stays in your gym's own account and is protected per-branch by role.
